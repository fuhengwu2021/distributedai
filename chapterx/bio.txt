Fuheng Wu is a Principal ML Tech Lead at Oracle Generative AI, specializing in distributed training, inference, and GPU systems for enterprise AI workloads. He has delivered core components of Oracle's large-scale vision and document-AI models and Oracle Code Assist (Oracle's AI coding copilot), and co-authored a Microsoft–Oracle blog on high-performance deep learning with ONNX Runtime. A graduate of the Singapore–MIT Alliance, where he studied linear algebra with Gilbert Strang at MIT, he began his career in applied machine learning at NetEase, then worked on Wall Street in quantitative finance—pricing convertible bonds at Bloomberg and developing algorithmic trading strategies at WorldQuant—before joining Uber, where he built distributed training systems on Michelangelo with Petastorm and Horovod.

Fuheng has hands-on experience across PyTorch Distributed, DeepSpeed, Megatron-style parallelism, Kubernetes GPU clusters, and production LLM serving. He has contributed to open-source projects including SGLang, vLLM, genai-bench, pyLLaMA, chatLLaMA, and HiQ, and is the author of *Mathematics for AI and Machine Learning*. He teaches Generative AI foundations at the McCombs School of Business, University of Texas at Austin, and mentors students preparing for AI olympiads. Writing as Xuan Xin, he is also the author of the poetic memoir *Above the Clouds: From Mountain Springs to the Edge of AI in Silicon Valley* and the music album *Let's Train The Model* (2024), which renders deep learning and AI concepts as music. Beyond technology, he is a Zen calligrapher and volunteer math tutor.
