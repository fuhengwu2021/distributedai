## Production Notes

- **Tone:** Confident, fast-paced, no jargon dump — this sells the book, it doesn't teach the chapter. Save the deep technical walkthrough for the conference talk (`distaisys-slides.md`).
- **Captions:** Amazon plays A+ videos muted by default — every VO line must also appear as on-screen text or captions.
- **Music:** Upbeat, tech-forward bed track under the whole video; duck slightly during VO.
- **Pacing check:** ~150 spoken words total at the scripted VO ≈ 70–75 seconds at a natural pace — trim Scene 4's chapter list first if the edit runs long.
- **Alt cut:** If Amazon caps hero video length, Scenes 1–3 + 6 alone make a clean 40-second teaser (drop Scenes 4–5).
