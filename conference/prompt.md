We are excited to welcome Henry Fuheng Wu, Executive Director on ML/AI & Senior Manager on Quant Analytics at Wells Fargo, as a speaker at the Global Data & AI Virtual Tech Conference 2026, presented by AI Every Time.

Henry is an AI leader, distributed systems expert, and author of Above The Clouds, Mathematics for AI and Machine Learning, and Distributed AI Systems. He is also a Judge of the International AI Innovation Olympiad and an Amazon Best-Selling Author in AI.

With experience across Wells Fargo, Oracle, Uber, Oscar Health, Bloomberg, Google, and WorldQuant, Henry has worked on production AI systems, large-scale inference, distributed model training, GPU workloads, ML infrastructure, quantitative analytics, and real-time data platforms.

In his session, “From Single GPU to Production Clusters: Building Distributed AI Systems for Training and Inference,” Henry will share a practical framework for moving AI systems from single-GPU prototypes to reliable production-scale clusters.

He will cover:

• Distributed training strategies
• PyTorch DDP and FSDP
• vLLM and SGLang for inference
• GPU memory and networking bottlenecks
• Benchmarking and observability
• Common production failure modes

Attendees will learn how to design AI infrastructure that is scalable, reliable, observable, and ready for real-world training and inference workloads.

This session is essential for AI engineers, ML infrastructure teams, data scientists, cloud architects, platform engineers, and leaders building production-scale AI systems.
